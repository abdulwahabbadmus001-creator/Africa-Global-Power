# Africa & Global Power

**Africa & Global Power** is an open-access publication repository dedicated to research and analysis on Africa's place in an evolving global order.

This repository serves as the official archive for all publications released under the Africa & Global Power platform. Each publication is version-controlled, openly accessible, and permanently preserved through GitHub.

---

## Mission

To provide evidence-based, accessible, and insightful analysis that helps readers better understand Africa's political, economic, technological, and strategic role in global affairs.

---

## Research Areas

- African Geopolitics
- International Relations
- Diplomacy & Foreign Policy
- Economics & Development
- Global Trade & Investment
- Artificial Intelligence
- Science & Technology
- Security & Defence
- Climate & Sustainability
- Governance & Public Policy
